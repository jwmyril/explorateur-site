In short

In 2016, the lowest value is recorded in La Victoire (9.1 %), the highest in Belladère (84.2 %). The median value is 54 %. 44 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Share of the commune's water points, among those tested, where WPdx finds fecal coliform — the mark of fecal contamination.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

SHARE OF TESTED POINTS, NOT OF ALL POINTS: only the commune's water points that were tested for fecal coliform count (2,974 of 6,276 nationally); an untested point enters neither as clean nor as contaminated. The test says fecal contamination is present at the tap when sampled, not that it is constant. Most surveys are from 2016: a past state, not today's. Computed only where at least ten points were tested. From 9% to 84% depending on the commune, with a median of 54%: on tested points, fecal contamination is the rule rather than the exception.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Share of the commune's water points, among those tested, where WPdx finds fecal coliform — the mark of fecal contamination.
Method
WPdx water points attached to the commune (centre inside the CNIGS polygon) whose `fecal_coliform_presence` field is « Present » divided by those that are « Present » or « Absent », times 100. Untested points are excluded. Published if at least ten of the commune's points were tested.
How to read it
High = more tested points contaminated
Comparability
Comparable between communes with at least ten tested points, at close survey dates (mostly 2016).
Unit
%
Reference year
2016
Source
Water Point Data Exchange (WPdx) — fecal coliform tests, Atmart share
