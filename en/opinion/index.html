Haiti Explorer

What Haitians think

51 questions from the AmericasBarometer survey (LAPOP), 2006 to 2023: trust, corruption, security, democracy, economy and hunger, participation — for Haiti, its five survey regions and the Dominican Republic.

Each value is an estimate on a 0 to 100 scale, computed by Atmart from LAPOP's microdata using the sample design (weights, strata, clusters). For a yes/no question, it is the percentage answering "yes". For a scale, 0 is the first possible answer and 100 the last: the meaning is given under each question. Hover over or tap a value to see its 95% confidence interval and the number of respondents.

The regions are the survey's five sampling strata, not departments. The survey allows no estimate by commune.

Watch for breaks: Haiti 2021 and 2023, and the Dominican Republic 2021, were interviewed by phone; the other waves face to face. A change between 2017 and 2021 may come from the method as much as from opinion. A question is estimated for a wave only if it appears in that wave's official questionnaire.

†: fewer than 100 respondents, fragile estimate.
