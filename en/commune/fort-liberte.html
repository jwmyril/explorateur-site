Fort-Liberté

Fort-Liberté is a commune of the Nord-Est department, in the arrondissement of Fort-Liberté. It covers 237.27 km² and has 4 communal sections. Its population is estimated at 40,129 inhabitants for 2024, that is 169.1 inhabitants per km².
