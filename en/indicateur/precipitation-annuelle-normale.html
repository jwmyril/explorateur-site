In short

In 2020, the lowest value is recorded in Bombardopolis (514.4 mm per year), the highest in Maniche (2,459 mm per year). The median value is 1,278 mm per year. All 140 communes have a published value.

Mean annual rainfall total over the 1991-2020 climate reference period.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

A thirty-year normal is not a forecast, and it hides year-to-year variability — which is precisely what an average erases.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Mean annual rainfall total over the 1991-2020 climate reference period.
Method
Mean of CHIRPS annual totals over 1991-2020, sampled on the pixels of the commune polygon.
How to read it
Neither good nor bad: read it alongside agriculture and risk
Comparability
Comparable between communes of the same reference year.
