In short

In 2020, the lowest value is recorded in Abricots (317 buildings), the highest in La Tortue (6,934 buildings). The median value is 1,482 buildings. 50 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Number of buildings that the design of ANARSE's PHARES programme plans to connect to the solar mini-grid assessed in the commune.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

A PROJECT, NOT A BUILT NETWORK. ANARSE had fifty rural sites assessed in 2020: these figures say what a design assigns to each site, not what exists on the ground. Only the fifty communes concerned have a value; the others were not assessed, which says nothing about their needs. Six sites were left without a building estimate.

Method

How to read it
High = more buildings planned for connection
Comparability
Comparable between the fifty assessed communes; elsewhere, the absence of a value says nothing about needs.
