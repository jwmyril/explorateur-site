In short

In 2015, the lowest value is recorded in Grand-Boucan (2,595 registered voters), the highest in Port-au-Prince (474,403 registered voters). The median value is 21,478 registered voters. All 140 communes have a published value.

Number of people on the commune's electoral list, according to the count published by the CEP in April 2015.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

A LIST IS NOT A POPULATION: the electoral roll, opened in 2005, has not been cleaned of people who died or left. In 2016 the European Union electoral expert mission estimated that 500,000 to 600,000 people should be removed, up to about 10% of the roll. The excess is not known commune by commune. It is also an April 2015 count: the list changed again before the 2015 and 2016 elections.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Number of people on the commune's electoral list, according to the count published by the CEP in April 2015.
Method
Reading of the CEP document “Voter registration (April 2015) — by department and by commune”. Each commune name is matched to the reference list; the sum of communes is checked against each department's stated total, and the sum of departments against the national total (5,835,295).
How to read it
Neutral — a count, not a performance
Comparability
Comparable between communes for the same count (April 2015); not as a measure of the adult population, since the list has not been cleaned.
Unit
registered voters
Reference year
2015
Source
CEP — Voter registration, April 2015 (Internet Archive copy)
