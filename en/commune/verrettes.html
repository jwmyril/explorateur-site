Verrettes

Verrettes is a commune of the Artibonite department, in the arrondissement of Saint-Marc. It covers 348.02 km² and has 6 communal sections. Its population is estimated at 146,485 inhabitants for 2024, that is 420.9 inhabitants per km².

This edition documents 86 indicators for this commune. 4 indicators remain undocumented here — they are named below, and an absence is not a zero.

Administrative completeness score: 80 %. It measures what we know about this commune, not what the commune is — a low score marks a documentary blind spot, not a poorly equipped territory.
