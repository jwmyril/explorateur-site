Share of the commune's recorded water points whose last reported status is “non-functional”.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

THE STATUS IS AS OLD AS THE LAST VISIT, which may go back several years. Computed only where at least ten points are recorded: below that, a share cannot be read.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Share of the commune's recorded water points whose last reported status is “non-functional”.
Method
“Non-functional” points ÷ all WPdx points in the commune × 100, if the commune has at least ten.
How to read it
High = more points out of service
Comparability
Comparable between communes with at least ten points, with similar visit dates.
Unit
%
Reference year
2026
Source
Water Point Data Exchange (WPdx) — recorded points, counted by Atmart
