Fund continuity, not closed data

Services and partnerships

The Haiti Explorer is free, account-free and tracker-free, and will stay that way. What is charged here is not access to the data: it is the work that makes it reliable, documented and usable — and the guarantee that it will still exist next year.

A rule that is not negotiable. No engagement makes public data less accessible. What one client funds benefits everyone: a gap filled for them is filled for all, and published the same day.

Pillar 1

Sustainable funding and services

Fund the continuity of the public good without turning the data into a closed product.

Sponsorships

Open a dataset, adopt a vintage, fill a gap, maintain a layer. The sponsor is named on the dataset sheet, with the date — and gains no exclusivity: the data is public from the day it is published.

What makes this measurable: the map « Where our information is weakest » names the communes where a sponsorship truly changes something. Today, 8 communes have two data families out of three in disagreement or unverifiable.

See sponsorable datasets

Engagements

Territorial diagnosis, quality assurance, reference harmonisation, accessibility, risk analysis, siting studies, dashboards.

What sets this work apart: every deliverable states what it does not allow you to conclude. A report with no « limits » section is not a short report, it is an incomplete one.

Atmart Verified

Audit of a dataset: provenance, quality, geocoding, internal consistency, consent, licence and documentation. The deliverable is a sheet following « Datasheets for Datasets » (Gebru et al.), Aether version, adapted to territorial data.

What exists nowhere else: completeness is recounted from the file at every generation, never copied from a note. And four sections are added to the original model — measured completeness, agreement between sources, legal passport, and « what this dataset does not allow you to conclude », the one no producer fills in spontaneously.

Service subscription

Analyst hours, training, custom maps, file checks, alerts and support. A monthly volume, not privileged access to the data.

Institutional instances

An instance of the Explorer for your internal data: visual identity, private roles, deployment and support. Your data stays yours; the engine is the same as the public one.

Rapid activation

Annual preparation before the cyclone season, and post-disaster response. Every activation produces a public-interest report: what one actor funds in an emergency stays readable by all.

Pillar 2

Partnerships, capacity and recognition

Build an ecosystem of producers, users, maintainers and funders that reduces dependence on Atmart alone. A public good that rests on one company is not a public good: it is a service with a breaking point.

  1. The annual consortium of Explorer Guardians

    Members who fund the core and sit on its governance. They do not decide the figures — the methods stay public and contestable — they decide the completeness priorities.

  2. Agreements with producers

    Public producers, universities, libraries, diaspora networks, sector organisations. The purpose of an agreement is not to obtain a file: it is to make a divergence repairable, on both sides.

  3. University labs

    Internships, reproduction of indicators, validation, translation and documentation. An indicator a student cannot reproduce is not an indicator, it is a claim.

  4. Digital Public Good application

    Starting from a gap analysis, then budgeting the annual review. We begin by measuring what is missing against the nine criteria — not by filing an application.

  5. Infrastructure programmes

    Apply only after eligibility is confirmed, and with a vendor exit plan written in advance. A dependency taken on without an exit is a permanent dependency.

  6. Caribbean replication

    To be assessed after the Haitian core proves sustainable. Expanding before being sustainable multiplies a fragility.

What is not for sale, and never will be

  • Access to the Explorer's public data — free, no account, no advertising tracker.
  • Exclusivity on a dataset: a sponsorship funds a publication, it never reserves its use.
  • Data whose licence forbids commercial use — two sources in the registry are in this case and are refused for engagements.
  • A figure without its source, its vintage and its limit.
  • An individual observation: everything is aggregated by commune, no person is identifiable.
