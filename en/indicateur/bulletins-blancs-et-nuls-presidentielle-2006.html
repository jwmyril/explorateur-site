In short

In 2006, the lowest value is recorded in Delmas (4.11 %), the highest in Beaumont (31.51 %). The median value is 16.4 %. 107 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Share of blank and spoiled ballots among all ballots counted in the commune in the first round of the presidential election of 7 February 2006.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

BLANK BALLOTS WERE AT THE HEART OF THE 2006 DISPUTE: at national level, the CEP distributed them among the candidates in proportion to their votes, which put the leader above 50%. The commune summaries do not make this distribution: this is the share BEFORE distribution. A blank ballot is a choice; a spoiled ballot may also be a filling error — both are added here because some summaries barely separate them. Summaries sometimes partial (see tally sheets processed); 32 communes without a value, and Bombardopolis set aside: its summary declares zero blank and zero spoiled ballots on 10 tally sheets, which reflects data entry, not the vote.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Share of blank and spoiled ballots among all ballots counted in the commune in the first round of the presidential election of 7 February 2006.
Method
(blank votes + spoiled votes) ÷ total votes × 100, read from the CEP's commune summary.
How to read it
Neutral — a blank ballot is a choice, a spoiled ballot may be an error
Comparability
Comparable between the communes covered, before the national distribution of blank ballots; not with the official national figure, which distributed them.
Unit
%
Reference year
2006
Source
CEP — EIS summaries of the presidential election of 7 February 2006 (Internet Archive copy)
