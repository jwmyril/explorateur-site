In short

In 2026, the lowest value is recorded in Paillant (0 km per km²), the highest in Grand Gosier (0.45 km per km²). The median value is 0.2 km per km². 130 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Kilometres of rivers in the HydroRIVERS network within the commune, per square kilometre of commune.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

A COMPUTED NETWORK, NOT A SURVEY: HydroRIVERS derives rivers from a ~500 m terrain model and keeps only those whose upstream basin exceeds 10 km² — short ravines are missing, and a river channelled through a city is not always there. Published only for communes of at least 50 km²: below that, a single segment of a few kilometres makes the figure.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Kilometres of rivers in the HydroRIVERS network within the commune, per square kilometre of commune.
Method
Each HydroRIVERS segment is matched to the commune containing its middle vertex; sum of the published lengths (LENGTH_KM) ÷ area (IND-GEO-001).
How to read it
High = denser river network
Comparability
Comparable between communes of at least 50 km²; says nothing about flow or available water.
Unit
km per km²
Reference year
2026
Source
HydroRIVERS / HydroSHEDS (Lehner and Grill 2013), Atmart calculation
