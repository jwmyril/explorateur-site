Côtes de Fer

Côtes de Fer is a commune of the Sud-Est department, in the arrondissement of Bainet. It covers 175.68 km² and has 6 communal sections. Its population is estimated at 63,370 inhabitants for 2024, that is 360.7 inhabitants per km².
