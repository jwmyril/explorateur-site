La Tortue

La Tortue is a commune of the Nord-Ouest department, in the arrondissement of Port-de-Paix. It covers 178.53 km² and has 2 communal sections. Its population is estimated at 40,156 inhabitants for 2024, that is 224.9 inhabitants per km².
