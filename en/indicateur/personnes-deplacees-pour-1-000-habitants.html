In short

In 2026, the lowest value is recorded in Pétion-Ville (0.41 per 1,000 residents), the highest in Grand-Boucan (701.24 per 1,000 residents). The median value is 76.1 per 1,000 residents. 139 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Internally displaced people present in the commune per 1,000 residents.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

The numerator (IOM, May 2026) and the denominator (COD-PS 2024 projection) are not from the same year, and the projection does not account for displacement itself. An order of magnitude, not an exact rate. Non-commercial use (IOM terms).

Indicator sheet

Definition
Internally displaced people present in the commune per 1,000 residents.
Method
Displaced people present (IND-SEC-003) ÷ population (IND-POP-001) × 1,000.
How to read it
High = heavier burden relative to population
Comparability
Comparable between covered communes, as an order of magnitude.
Unit
per 1,000 residents
