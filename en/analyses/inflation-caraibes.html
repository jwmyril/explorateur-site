In 2025, prices rose by 28.6% in Haiti, the steepest increase in the Caribbean

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-18 · Haiti in the Caribbean

In 2025, Haiti's consumer price index rose by 28.6% over one year, according to the International Monetary Fund as reported by the World Bank.

Among the 12 other Caribbean countries that report this figure for 2025, the steepest rise is in Suriname (9.2%). In the Dominican Republic, it is 3.9%.

This is not a one-year accident: since 2016, prices in Haiti have risen by more than 10% every year. The rise peaked at 36.8% in 2023.

It is a national twelve-month average: it says neither which goods rose most nor where. IHSI also publishes an index by region, which the Explorer carries; the two figures can differ depending on the period of calculation.

The group compares the sixteen independent Caribbean countries, three of them on the mainland (Belize, Guyana, Suriname); Puerto Rico and dependent territories are not included.
