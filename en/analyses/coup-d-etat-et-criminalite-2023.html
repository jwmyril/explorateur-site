In 2023, 67% of Haitians would find a military coup justified when crime is high

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-19

The question is hypothetical: "would a military coup be justified if there were a lot of crime?" In 2023, 67% of Haitians surveyed said yes. The figure was 19% in 2012, 29% in 2014 and 29% in 2017.

The metropolitan area stands apart: 47% in 2023, against 70% to 75% in the four other regions. It is the only regional gap the margins of error allow us to assert.

In the Dominican Republic, the same year, 35% said yes.

Two cautions. The 2023 survey was conducted by phone, the earlier ones face to face: the jump since 2017 mixes a change of opinion with a change of method. And the question was put to only part of the sample (1,078 people). It measures a disposition, not support for an actual plan.
