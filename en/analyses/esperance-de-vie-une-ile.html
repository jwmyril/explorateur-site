On the same island, life expectancy is 8.8 years shorter in Haiti than in the Dominican Republic

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-18 · Haiti in the Caribbean

In 2024, life expectancy at birth is 65.1 years in Haiti and 73.9 years in the Dominican Republic, according to the World Bank. The two countries share the same island and have roughly the same population: 11.9 and 11.5 million in 2025.

Of the 16 Caribbean countries compared, Haiti has the shortest life expectancy; the next country, Guyana, is at 70.3 years.

Haiti's curve fell from 64.3 years in 2019 to 62.6 years in 2021, during the covid-19 pandemic, before rising again.

Life expectancy at birth summarises one year's mortality at all ages; it does not predict how long a person will live. For Haiti, lacking complete civil registration, it rests on United Nations estimates.

The group compares the sixteen independent Caribbean countries, three of them on the mainland (Belize, Guyana, Suriname); Puerto Rico and dependent territories are not included.
