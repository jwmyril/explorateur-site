In one year, prices rose by 16.9 to 19.8% depending on the region

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-18

Over twelve months, IHSI's consumer price index rises by 16.9 % to 19.8 % across the 5 regions of its breakdown. The smallest rise is in the region of Cap-Haïtien, the largest in that of Croix-Des-Bouquets.

The gap between regions is small: 2.9 points. That is information in itself: the rise hits the whole country, and no region escapes it.

Each commune carries its region's value: this is not a commune-level reading.
