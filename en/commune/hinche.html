Hinche

Hinche is a commune of the Centre department, in the arrondissement of Hinche. It covers 582.51 km² and has 4 communal sections. Its population is estimated at 163,137 inhabitants for 2024, that is 280.1 inhabitants per km².

This edition documents 119 indicators for this commune. 4 indicators remain undocumented here — they are named below, and an absence is not a zero.

Administrative completeness score: 80 %. It measures what we know about this commune, not what the commune is — a low score marks a documentary blind spot, not a poorly equipped territory.
