8 communes are more than ten minutes' drive from a hospital

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-18

The median road travel time to the nearest hospital exceeds ten minutes in 8 communes, and five minutes in 25. The longest is recorded in Bombardopolis (21.6 min). Half of the communes are within 2.2 min.

The clock starts at the roadside. The walk from home, often the longest part of the journey in rural sections, is not counted; it also assumes a passable road and an available vehicle.

“Hospital” follows the tag placed in OpenStreetMap, not a ministry licence: a short time does not guarantee care.
