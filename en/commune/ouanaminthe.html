Ouanaminthe

Ouanaminthe is a commune of the Nord-Est department, in the arrondissement of Ouanaminthe. It covers 203.22 km² and has 5 communal sections. Its population is estimated at 123,320 inhabitants for 2024, that is 606.8 inhabitants per km².
