Trees cover more than half of the territory in 52 communes

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-18

According to the European Space Agency's satellite observation (2021), trees cover more than 50% of the surface in 52 communes, and less than 10% in 11. The share ranges from 0.3 % in Desdunes to 90.2 % in Chambellan.

This figure far exceeds the national forest area usually quoted. The two do not measure the same thing: the satellite counts orchards, hedges, Creole gardens and dense bush; forest statistics only keep forest.

It is a snapshot of a single year: it measures neither deforestation nor regrowth.
