In short

In 2026, the lowest value is recorded in 16 communes (1 level), the highest in 2 communes (4 level). The median value is 2 level. 47 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Average, over the commune's assessed communal sections, of the access-constraint level given by OCHA's Access Working Group: from 1 (least constrained) to 4 in this round.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

FORTY-SEVEN COMMUNES OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY, AND A COMMUNE WITHOUT A RATING IS NOT A COMMUNE WHERE AID GETS THROUGH EASILY: the Access Working Group assessed only 210 of the country's 570 communal sections. The remaining 93 communes have no value, never a zero. The rating is humanitarian experts' judgement of their own ability to reach people — not a measure of the insecurity residents live through. It is also the Explorateur's most perishable figure: a June 2026 round that the next edition will replace. An average smooths: a commune with one very constrained section and three quiet ones reads as moderate — read the most constrained section too (IND-SEC-007).

Indicator sheet

Definition
Average, over the commune's assessed communal sections, of the access-constraint level given by OCHA's Access Working Group: from 1 (least constrained) to 4 in this round.
Method
Sheet DB_Severity_ZMPP of OCHA's workbook: mean of Overall_SeverityLevel over the assessed sections, checked against the pivot table the producer ships in the same file.
How to read it
High = aid reaches residents with more difficulty
Comparability
Comparable between the 47 assessed communes, for this round; a commune without a rating is not a better-off commune.
