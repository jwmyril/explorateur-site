In 2026, the lowest value is recorded in Terre Neuve (0.06 objects per 1,000 residents), the highest in Croix-Des-Bouquets (50.08 objects per 1,000 residents). The median value is 1 objects per 1,000 residents. All 140 communes have a published value.

Number of objects mapped in OpenStreetMap (all points of interest) per 1,000 residents of the commune.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

READ ALONGSIDE ANY INDICATOR DRAWN FROM OPENSTREETMAP: without it, a poorly equipped commune cannot be told apart from a poorly mapped one. The range runs from 0.1 to 50 objects per 1,000 residents. It measures contributors' effort, not the territory's wealth: an area mapped after an earthquake or a humanitarian project is better covered than its neighbour.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Number of objects mapped in OpenStreetMap (all points of interest) per 1,000 residents of the commune.
Method
All objects of the HOT export matched to the commune ÷ population (IND-POP-001) × 1,000. Twenty-seven objects without a p-code are counted nowhere.
How to read it
High = better mapped commune
Comparability
Comparable between communes for the same extract.
Unit
objects per 1,000 residents
Reference year
2026
Source
OpenStreetMap via HOT (ODbL) — points of interest, counted by Atmart
