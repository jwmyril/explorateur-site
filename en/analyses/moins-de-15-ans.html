In half of the communes, more than one inhabitant in three is under 15

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-18

In 2024, the share of under-15s ranges from 22.4 % in Port-au-Prince to 42.5 % in Thomassique. The median commune has 35 %.

The five lowest shares: Port-au-Prince, Carrefour, Pétion-Ville, Grande Saline, Cité Soleil. The five highest: Thomassique, Cerca La Source, Fonds-Verrettes, Roche à Bâteau, Cerca Carvajal.

These are projections: the last census dates from 2003, and the 2024 estimate extends its trends. It is fit to compare communes, not to count pupils.
