What every licence forbids

Reselling or redistributing the dataset as is; removing source credits; using it for unsolicited canvassing; and any attempt to re-identify individuals. The detail is in the trust charter.

Pricing principle

We do not charge for rows, we charge for a transformation

A 200-row file that had to be cross-referenced across three institutions is worth more than a 50,000-row export grabbed in one click. The price follows six factors:

  • Scarcity — does the data exist elsewhere, in a usable form?
  • Preparation — how many hours of cleaning, cross-referencing and checking?
  • Refresh — is the dataset maintained, and how often?
  • Readability — dictionary, documentation, ready-to-use formats.
  • Depth — number of territories, years, variables.
  • Time saved — what the same work would cost you in-house.

How we work

From the question to the deliverable

  1. You describe the decision to be made — not the data you want, the decision. That is what determines everything else.
  2. We answer within 48 working hours — what already exists, what has to be built, what exists nowhere.
  3. Demonstration before commitment — a real extract, on your own perimeter, so you can judge relevance.
  4. Packaged proposal — scope, licence, deadline, firm price. No surprise hourly billing.
  5. Documented delivery — the files, the Fèy Done, the variable dictionary, the reading note.
  6. Follow-up — one question on usage stays included for 30 days.
