In short

In 2023, the lowest value is recorded in La Chapelle (0), the highest in Saint-Marc (53). The median value is 15. 14 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Number of health facilities with public status.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

“Mixed” status is not counted as public.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Number of health facilities with public status.
Method
Count of facilities whose source status is “Public”.
How to read it
Numerator of the share of public facilities; published separately so that this rate can be recomputed at any level.
Comparability
Same coverage limitations as recorded facilities.
