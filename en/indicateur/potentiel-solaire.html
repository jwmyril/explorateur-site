In short

In 2020, the lowest value is recorded in Moron (1,431 kWh per kWp per year), the highest in Grande Saline (1,859 kWh per kWp per year). The median value is 1,674 kWh per kWp per year. All 140 communes have a published value.

Photovoltaic output expected from a one-kilowatt-peak installation over a year, averaged across the commune's pixels.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

IT IS A RESOURCE, NOT EQUIPMENT: this figure says what a panel would produce, not what is installed. Differences between communes are small in a country this small and this sunny — a ranking on this indicator separates values that sit within a few per cent of each other. Relief matters more than latitude: the shadow of the mornes makes the difference, and it is averaged here over the whole commune.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Photovoltaic output expected from a one-kilowatt-peak installation over a year, averaged across the commune's pixels.
Method
Mean of the Global Solar Atlas PVOUT values over every pixel falling inside the commune outline.
How to read it
High = better potential
Comparability
Comparable between communes, but the gap is small across Haiti: a ranking would exaggerate differences the resource does not carry.
Unit
kWh per kWp per year
Reference year
2020
