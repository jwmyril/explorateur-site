In short

In 2024, the lowest value is recorded in 39 communes (1 phase (1 to 5)), the highest in Croix-Des-Bouquets (4 phase (1 to 5)). The median value is 1 phase (1 to 5). 64 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Phase assigned to the commune by Haiti's first IPC acute malnutrition analysis: 1 = acceptable, 2 = alert, 3 = serious, 4 = critical, 5 = extremely critical.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

A PHASE, NOT A RATE: it classifies the severity affecting at least 20% of children, based on the January-March 2023 SMART survey and other evidence. ONLY 64 COMMUNES HAVE A VALUE: the Ouest, Sud-Est, Nord-Est, Nord-Ouest and Nippes were analysed commune by commune; the Artibonite, Centre, Grande Anse, Nord and Sud were analysed as a whole, so their communes have no phase of their own. Plaisance du Sud and Grand-Boucan were analysed together. Current situation, not the projection.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Phase assigned to the commune by Haiti's first IPC acute malnutrition analysis: 1 = acceptable, 2 = alert, 3 = serious, 4 = critical, 5 = extremely critical.
Method
Reading of the report's “For the current period” paragraph, which lists the communes in each phase; each name is matched to the reference list, and the total must come to 63 units (64 communes), otherwise the calculation stops.
How to read it
High = more serious nutrition situation
Comparability
Comparable between the 64 communes analysed at commune level; not with any other.
Unit
phase (1 to 5)
