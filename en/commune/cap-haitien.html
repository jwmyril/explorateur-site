Cap-Haïtien

Cap-Haïtien is a commune of the Nord department, in the arrondissement of Cap-Haïtien. It covers 54.11 km² and has 3 communal sections. Its population is estimated at 303,590 inhabitants for 2024, that is 5,611 inhabitants per km².
