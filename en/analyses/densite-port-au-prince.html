Port-au-Prince has 78 times more inhabitants per km² than the median commune

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-18

In 2024, Port-au-Prince holds 27,087 inhab./km². Haiti's median commune has 347.4 inhab./km²: the capital is 78 times denser. Of the 140 communes, 8 exceed 1,000 inhabitants per km², 6 of them in the Ouest department.

These figures divide an estimated population by an official area. They describe an average over the whole commune, mountains and uninhabitable land included: they say neither where the dense neighbourhoods are nor whether a commune lacks services.

Gressier is left out of the extremes: its population estimate is contradicted by the two other sources.
