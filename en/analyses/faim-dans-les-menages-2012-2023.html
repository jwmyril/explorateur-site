In 2023, 78% of Haitians said their household had run out of food, up from 42% in 2012

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-19

Asked "in the past three months, did your household run out of food?", 78% of Haitians surveyed in 2023 said yes. The figure was 62% in 2021 and 42% in 2012, the first time the question was asked.

No region is spared: in 2023 the share ranges from 71% (North) to 80% (South). In 2012 the gap was much wider, from 22% (North) to 61% (South). Food shortage has spread to the whole country.

In the Dominican Republic, the same question gives 42% in 2023, against 34% in 2012.

A caution: the 2021 and 2023 surveys were conducted by phone, the 2012 one face to face, and part of the gap may come from the method. A household without a phone could not answer in 2023: the figure says nothing about them.
