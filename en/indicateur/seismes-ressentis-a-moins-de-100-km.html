In short

In 2026, the lowest value is recorded in 2 communes (31 since 1911), the highest in Pointe à Raquette (222 since 1911). The median value is 105.5 since 1911. All 140 communes have a published value.

Number of earthquakes whose epicentre lies less than 100 km from the centre of the commune, since 1911.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

A count of PAST events is not a probabilistic hazard. The density of the measurement network has increased greatly: recent decades count better, not necessarily more.
