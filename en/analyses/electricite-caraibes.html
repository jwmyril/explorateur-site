Almost one person in two in Haiti lives without electricity, a situation unique in the Caribbean

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-18 · Haiti in the Caribbean

In 2024, 53.9% of Haiti's population has access to electricity, according to the World Bank. In other words, 46.1% of the population goes without it.

In the 15 other Caribbean countries that report this figure for 2024, access ranges from 93% (Guyana) to 100%. In the Dominican Republic, the other country on the island, it reaches 99.5%.

The share has nonetheless grown: it was 40.8% in 2015, a gain of 13.1 points.

“Having access” says nothing about reliability: a household connected to the grid but without power most of the day counts as having access. Values come from household surveys, completed by estimates in years without a survey.

The group compares the sixteen independent Caribbean countries, three of them on the mainland (Belize, Guyana, Suriname); Puerto Rico and dependent territories are not included.
