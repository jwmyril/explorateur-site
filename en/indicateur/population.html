In 2024, the lowest value is recorded in Grand-Boucan (6,460 inhabitants), the highest in Port-au-Prince (926,906 inhabitants). The median value is 47,137 inhabitants. All 140 communes have a published value. Gressier, whose population estimate is contradicted by the two other sources, is left out of these extremes.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Estimated resident population of the territorial entity.
Method
2024 demographic projection by sex and age (COD-PS, UNFPA), built on the COD-AB CNIGS 2018 boundaries — an estimate, not a count: the last census dates from 2003.
How to read it
Denominator of most other indicators: its quality conditions theirs.
Comparability
A methodological break is likely between the 2003 census and recent estimates — to be flagged.
