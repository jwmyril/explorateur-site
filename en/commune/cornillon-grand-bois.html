Cornillon / Grand Bois

Cornillon / Grand Bois is a commune of the Ouest department, in the arrondissement of Croix-Des-Bouquets. It covers 224.32 km² and has 5 communal sections. Its population is estimated at 66,185 inhabitants for 2024, that is 295 inhabitants per km².

This edition documents 97 indicators for this commune. 10 indicators remain undocumented here — they are named below, and an absence is not a zero.

Administrative completeness score: 60 %. It measures what we know about this commune, not what the commune is — a low score marks a documentary blind spot, not a poorly equipped territory.
