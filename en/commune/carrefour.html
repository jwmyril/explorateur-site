Carrefour

Carrefour is a commune of the Ouest department, in the arrondissement of Port-au-Prince. It covers 164.42 km² and has 13 communal sections. Its population is estimated at 460,506 inhabitants for 2024, that is 2,801 inhabitants per km².

This edition documents 76 indicators for this commune. 10 indicators remain undocumented here — they are named below, and an absence is not a zero.
