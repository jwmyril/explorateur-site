In short

In 2015, the lowest value is recorded in Cité Soleil (7.59 %), the highest in Dame Marie (45.54 %). The median value is 23.2 %. 66 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Share of valid votes (choices expressed and “no candidate”) won by the slate elected to the town hall, municipal elections of 25 October 2015.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

ONE ROUND, FIRST PAST THE POST: a slate can win with a small share of the vote when lists are numerous. The figure measures how dispersed the vote was, not the legitimacy or the work of the elected team. ONLY 66 COMMUNES OUT OF 140: the folder published by the CEP is titled “66 Communes”, and its page footers announce 69 pages — three are missing. The other communes are not at zero: their page was not published. THE PAGES ARE SCANS, keyed in by hand by Atmart; each page is checked against its own printed percentage, and tally sheets and spoiled ballots were read twice. Only INCLUDED tally sheets are counted (see the neighbouring indicator).

Indicator sheet

Definition
Share of valid votes (choices expressed and “no candidate”) won by the slate elected to the town hall, municipal elections of 25 October 2015.
Method
Winning slate's votes ÷ (choices expressed + “no candidate”) × 100, as printed; the recomputed value must match the CEP percentage within 0.01 point, or the entry is refused.
How to read it
Neutral — a low score means a dispersed vote, not a bad mayor
Comparability
Comparable between the 66 published communes for the same election; a gap mostly reflects the number of competing lists.
Unit
%
Reference year
2015
Source
CEP — final results of the 25 October 2015 municipal elections (public CEP folder, keyed in by Atmart)
