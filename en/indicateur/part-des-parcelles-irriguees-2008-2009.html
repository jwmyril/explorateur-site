In short

In 2009, the lowest value is recorded in Grand-Boucan (0 %), the highest in Chantal (41.92 %). The median value is 0.8 %. 41 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Share of the commune's plots that the census reports as irrigated.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

A 2008-2009 CENSUS, NEVER UPDATED. It is exhaustive and reliable for its year, and that year precedes the 2010 earthquake, Hurricane Matthew (2016) and the displacement since 2021. THREE DEPARTMENTS ONLY: the Nippes, Sud and Grande-Anse reports are available; the seven others remain to be found. The original unit is the carreau (1 carreau = 1.29 hectares). No licence is declared by the ministry: published with citation, never sold. Irrigation in 2009 says nothing about the state of the systems today.
