Saut d'Eau

Saut d'Eau is a commune of the Centre department, in the arrondissement of Mirebalais. It covers 171.59 km² and has 4 communal sections. Its population is estimated at 52,389 inhabitants for 2024, that is 305.3 inhabitants per km².
