In short

In 2015, the lowest value is recorded in 3 communes (1 hibiscus (1 to 5)), the highest in 2 communes (5 hibiscus (1 to 5)). The median value is 3 hibiscus (1 to 5). 39 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

The highest rating obtained by an establishment of the commune in the Ministry of Tourism's Hibiscus classification.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

A MAXIMUM IS NOT AN AVERAGE: a commune with 5 hibiscus may have a single classified establishment and all others at 1. The scale itself is not published by the Haitian State — it belongs to the Corporation de l'industrie touristique du Québec — so we cannot say what each rank requires. Only six communes carry an establishment at 4 or 5.
