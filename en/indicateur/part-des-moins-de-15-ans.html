In short

In 2024, the lowest value is recorded in Port-au-Prince (22.4 %), the highest in Thomassique (42.5 %). The median value is 35 %. All 140 communes have a published value. Gressier, whose population estimate is contradicted by the two other sources, is left out of these extremes.

Share of the under-15 population in the total.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

Every value here derives from a projection (COD-PS 2024), not a count: status E, never O — the last census dates from 2003. The source is five-year banded: no intermediate age boundary (5-18, median age) is drawn from it, since splitting a band would mean inventing a distribution. The top band is open-ended (80 and over). Known source anomaly: COD-PS 2024 assigns 3,987 inhabitants to Gressier (HT0116), out of proportion with its neighbours; its age counts inherit the anomaly and should not be read. Finally, the communal breakdown is more fragile than the total: the sex ratio at birth is biologically stable (101.8 men per 100 women nationally in the 0-4 band), yet it spreads from 89 to 116 across communes. An age-and-sex structure should be read with caution at commune level, and a single band's gap means nothing on its own.

How to read it
High = young population. Recomputed from the totals at each level of aggregation, never by averaging communal shares.
Comparability
Comparable across all 140 communes of the 2018 CNIGS reference: the projection covers the whole country, with no blind spot. Comparable over time only against another COD-PS reference year built on the same boundaries.
