In short

In 2026, the lowest value is recorded in 6 communes (1 water points), the highest in Hinche (380 water points). The median value is 63.5 water points. 74 of 140 communes have a published value; for the others, the figure is not zero: it is not known.

Number of water points (wells, boreholes, protected springs, standpipes) recorded in the commune by organisations reporting to the Water Point Data Exchange.

What this indicator does not allow you to conclude

A RECORD, NOT A CENSUS: WPdx gathers what projects have reported, at different dates. A missing commune was not surveyed — it is not without water. The national system (DINEPA's SIEPA) counts far more points but does not publish them.

Indicator sheet

Definition
Number of water points (wells, boreholes, protected springs, standpipes) recorded in the commune by organisations reporting to the Water Point Data Exchange.
Method
Sum of functional, non-functional and unknown-status WPdx points matched to the commune (family “eau_wpdx” in atmart_infrastructures_communes_HT.csv).
How to read it
High = more points recorded
Comparability
Hardly comparable: depends on whether projects reporting to WPdx were active.
Unit
water points
