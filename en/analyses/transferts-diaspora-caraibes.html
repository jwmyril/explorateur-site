Diaspora remittances amount to 16.9% of Haiti's GDP, the largest share in the Caribbean in 2024

By Atmart Data · 2026-09-18 · Haiti in the Caribbean

In 2024, money sent by migrants to their families amounts to 16.9% of Haiti's gross domestic product, according to the World Bank. The share was 14.8% in 2015; it peaked at 22.5% in 2020.

Among the 12 other Caribbean countries that report this figure for 2024, the closest is Jamaica (16.2%). In the Dominican Republic, remittances amount to 9.1% of GDP.

Haiti's lead is slim: Jamaica even overtook it in 2015, 2016, 2021 and 2022.

A share of GDP also depends on GDP itself: when the economy shrinks, the remittance share rises even if amounts do not change. Amounts received by department, drawn from household surveys, are in the Explorer.

The group compares the sixteen independent Caribbean countries, three of them on the mainland (Belize, Guyana, Suriname); Puerto Rico and dependent territories are not included.
